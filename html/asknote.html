A closer look at AskNote

Ask AI about your notes, right on your Mac

Chat directly with your own notes from a full desktop window. AskNote reads across your library and answers based on what you've already written — no manual searching required.

Record meetings without leaving your desk

Capture a meeting, lecture, or call and let on-device AI transcribe and summarize it — fully offline, with nothing leaving your Mac.

Auto-summary, auto-tagging

AskNote automatically summarizes, tags, and links related notes together, so your library stays organized without extra effort.

Choose your own AI

Stay fully on-device with Apple Intelligence or Google LiteRT-LM, or connect your own key for Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT, and more.
