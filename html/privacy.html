Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

Introduction

AinzApp ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile applications (WeeklyTodo, TagNote, and AskNote) and related services.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We do not collect any personal information from our users. Our apps are designed to work without requiring personal data collection.

Usage Data

We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve our apps, including:

  • App usage patterns (anonymized)
  • Crash reports and error logs (no personal data)
  • Performance data (device type, app version)

How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect personal information, we use only anonymous usage data to:

  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Analyze app usage patterns to enhance features
  • Ensure app stability and reliability

AI Features and Third-Party Services

AskNote lets you choose between on-device AI and cloud AI services to process your notes and questions.

  • If you choose on-device AI, such as Apple Intelligence or Google LiteRT-LM, all processing happens locally on your device, and your notes or questions are never sent externally.
  • If you choose a cloud AI service, such as Gemini, Claude, or ChatGPT, the questions you enter and the related note content are sent to the cloud service you have connected. This content is processed by that service provider in accordance with its own privacy policy and terms of service, and we do not store or access the content transmitted in this process.

Please also review the privacy policy of any cloud AI service provider you choose to use, as data processing for that service is governed by their policies.

Use of Third-Party AI Services

If you directly select a cloud AI service (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google) in Settings, using the "Ask AI" feature will send the question you entered and the title and body of any notes referenced in the conversation to the server of the selected service. This transmission occurs only when you select that service and agree to the in-app confirmation step; if you use on-device AI (Apple Intelligence, Google LiteRT-LM), no data is ever sent outside your device. Transmitted data is used solely for the purpose of generating a response, and the respective service provider's own privacy policy also applies.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data to share or disclose. However, as described above under "AI Features and Third-Party Services," if you choose to connect a cloud AI service, your questions and note content may be sent to that service. Any anonymous usage statistics are used solely for app improvement purposes.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. However, if you have any concerns about our privacy practices, please contact us.

Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect personal information from any users, including children, there are no special considerations for children's privacy beyond standard app safety measures.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
